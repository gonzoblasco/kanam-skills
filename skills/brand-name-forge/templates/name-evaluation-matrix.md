## Criteria Definitions

- **Memorable:** Does it stick? Easy to recall after one hearing?
- **Pronounceable:** Can people say it after reading it? No ambiguity?
- **Meaningful:** Does it convey something about the brand? Even if abstract?
- **Distinctive:** Stands out from competitors? Not generic?
- **Domain available:** .com or acceptable TLD available at reasonable price?
- **Trademark safe:** No obvious conflicts in the category?
- **Cross-language safe:** No unfortunate meanings in key markets?
- **Scalable:** Works for future products/expansion? Not too narrow?
