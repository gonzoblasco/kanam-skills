# Naming Principles

## The 7 Qualities of a Great Name

1. **Memorable** - Sticks in the mind after one hearing
2. **Pronounceable** - No ambiguity in how to say it
3. **Meaningful** - Carries or suggests meaning (even if abstract)
4. **Distinctive** - Stands out from the category
5. **Short** - 2-3 syllables ideal, 4 max
6. **Ownable** - Domain and trademark available
7. **Scalable** - Won't limit future growth

## Name Archetypes

| Archetype | Strategy | Best For |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| **Descriptive** | Says what it does | B2B, utilitarian products |
| **Evocative** | Suggests a feeling | Consumer, lifestyle brands |
| **Invented** | Made-up word | Tech, global brands |
| **Experiential** | Connected to user experience | Services, apps |
| **Founder** | Named after person | Professional services, luxury |

## What to Avoid

- **Generic terms** - "Quality Software Solutions Inc."
- **Trendy spellings** - "Lyft" was clever in 2012, now it's tired
- **Hard to spell** - If you have to spell it every time, it's a bad name
- **Limiting** - "MobileAppCo" when you might expand to web
- **Negative connotations** - Check in all key languages
- **Too similar to competitors** - "Facepage" won't work

## Domain Strategy

1. **Exact match .com** - ideal but rare
2. **Get[Name].com** or **[Name]App.com** - acceptable
3. **[Name].io / .co / .dev** - good for tech
4. **[Name].ai** - good for AI products
5. **Avoid:** .biz, .info, hyphenated domains, misspellings

## Quick Checks

Before falling in love with a name:

1. Google it - what comes up?
2. USPTO search - any live trademarks in your category?
3. Say it out loud 5 times - still sounds good?
4. Tell someone the name - can they spell it back?
5. Check urban dictionary - any unfortunate meanings?
6. Check in Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Chinese - any issues?
